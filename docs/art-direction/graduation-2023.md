# Graduation Day, Hanoi (2023-08-29) — art direction

- **Event basis:** University commencement at the Academy of Cryptography Techniques (*Học viện Kỹ thuật Mật mã*, Hanoi), Tuesday, August 29, 2023. Curated from Huy's shared 102-item Google Photos album (`Tốt nghiệp 29/08/2023`).
- **Photo selection & measurements:** 11 WebP photographs optimized from original frames (1600×1200 landscape covers/groups; 1400×1867 & 944×1259 3:4 portraits; 158–658 KB each). Cover (`campus-crew.webp`) sampled at 200 px averages neutral cool daylight RGB `161/165/168`, framed around deep navy (`#162033`) and crimson (`#9e2330`) graduation gowns.
- **Design decision (`storyStyle: "commencement"`):** Warm archival parchment (`#f2ede4`), academic navy ink (`#162033`), ceremonial crimson metadata and chapter dividers (`#9e2330`), Georgia serif display and body copy, and level un-rotated framing so family and group portraits remain composed.
- **Narrative arc:** Wide group cover → Auditorium ceremony & parents → Solo joy & quiet candid reading → Friends along the track & KMA tower → Wry epilogue at home (red *Bằng Kỹ Sư* as mousepad paired with sunflowers on the stairs).
- **Reading check:** Verified `alt` text on all 11 images, keyboard-accessible lightbox with prev/next counter, lazy loading on below-the-fold frames, and mobile single-column stacking.
