# Journeys 2023–2026 — Per-Destination Art Direction & 3D Atmosphere System

Each of the 6 real travel albums from Huy's Google Photos archives was visually audited (36-frame contact sheets sampled across every trip) and curated into 11–12 optimized WebP photographs stored under `src/assets/places/<slug>/`. Each destination uses its own palette, font pairing, 3D atmospheric canvas, and interactive photo-viewing choreography (`Photo3DShowcase`, `PhotoDeck3D`, `PhotoStrip`, `PhotoPair`, and the pan/zoom Museum Lightbox).
