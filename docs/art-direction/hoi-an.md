# Hội An — preview art direction

- **Place evidence:** [Vietnam Tourism](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/the-best-ways-to-explore-the-ancient-town-of-hoi-an/) highlights yellow shop fronts, lanterns, and boats on the river. Accessed 2026-11-14.
- **Photo basis:** five location-identified CC0 photographs in the preview: riverside street, night boats, lantern shop, lantern detail, and market. Their source pages are linked in the article captions.
- **Measurement:** the 1200 × 800 street cover averages RGB 114/113/108 at 200 px; the night river image averages RGB 38/34/32. The strong light-to-dark shift supports an ink background and amber display lettering.
- **Design:** bold sans display type, tighter text rhythm, and a denser mosaic that lets pools of lantern light punctuate the dark page. Photos have individual captions and source links.
- **Search trends:** no reliable query-volume or visitor metric was used for this preview. Research and date such a metric when real trip content is supplied.
