# Đà Lạt — preview art direction

- **Place evidence:** [Vietnam Tourism](https://www.vietnam.travel/things-to-do/da-lat-enjoy-four-seasons-day) describes hills with pines, lakes, flower gardens, and changing light through the day. Accessed 2026-11-14.
- **Photo basis:** four location-identified CC0 photographs in the preview: aerial lake and hills, a lakeside hill, flowers, and cultivated slopes. Their source pages are linked in the article captions.
- **Measurement:** the 1438 × 899 aerial cover sampled at 200 px averages RGB 129/144/142. This muted, cool balance supports a pale green paper color. The cover and three gallery images create four distinct views.
- **Design:** Georgia serif, generous line height, wide spacing between photos, and an asymmetrical sequence that alternates landscape and detail. Subtle vertical reveals follow the unhurried visual rhythm; reduced-motion users see static photos.
- **Search trends:** no reliable query-volume or visitor metric was used for this preview. Research and date such a metric when real trip content is supplied.
