Hoi An changes character the minute the sky turns deep indigo. Daytime details recede, and every shopfront, boat, and eaves line starts speaking through warm pools of light.

<Photo src={river} alt="Lantern-lit wooden boats drifting across the Thu Bon River at night" caption="Warm light drifting across dark river water" credit="lumoplank · CC0" source="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hoi_An_-_HoiAn1441.jpg" layout="full" />

## Beyond the water, a canopy of glow

Step a few paces off the embankment and the scale tightens immediately. Silk frames, bamboo ribs, and tiled roofs pull the scene closer until color and material fill the entire frame.
