Coming back to a place you first visited years earlier changes how you look at it. In February 2023, Ta Xua was a frantic chase through thick winter fog; in November 2025, it opened up warm, crystalline, and completely unhurried.

## Cobalt sky over Thao Nguyen

No wall of mist this time — just endless rolling green pastureland under a deep November sky, where tiny distant silhouettes dotted the hillside and the wind sounded like tall grass breathing.
