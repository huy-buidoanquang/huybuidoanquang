Some trips are about chasing a view; this one was about packing a truck bed full of supplies, driving through December rain into the mountains of Dien Bien, and lending a hand at a remote highland school.

## The long road west

The journey began with cardboard cartons stacked onto pickup trucks, followed by hours of foggy night highway where amber town lamps blurred in the drizzle. By morning, wild yellow *dã quỳ* blossoms lined the mountain curves, and asphalt eventually narrowed into steep, mud-rutted village tracks.

## Hands on deck & shared tables

At the village school, everyone fell into rhythm — hauling cartons through narrow doorways, setting up clean water storage, sharing steaming bowls around low tables inside a wooden stilt house, and pausing whenever curious kids rolled past on tiny tricycles.

## When the afternoon light walked in

Before heading back down the mountain, the late sun slanted straight through the freshly opened classroom windows — casting warm squares of light across neat wooden desks and clean tiled floors. That quiet frame said more than any speech could.
