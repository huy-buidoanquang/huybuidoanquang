February in Ta Xua moves on weather's terms. One minute the valley below disappears beneath a motionless ocean of white; ten minutes later the wind parts the veil and exposes every razor fold of Son La.

## Above the cloud line

Waking up cold in the highlands pays off the instant you step onto the ridge deck and realize the sky isn't overhead anymore — it is pooled directly beneath your boots.
