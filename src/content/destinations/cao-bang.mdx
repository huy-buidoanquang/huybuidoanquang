Riding into Cao Bang over the April 30 holiday feels like rolling straight into a green limestone valley dressed in red — everywhere the karst peaks rise straight up, Vietnamese flags snap in the mountain wind.

## Two wheels north

Between rain-poncho stops under roadside awnings and wooden stilt-house courtyards tucked beneath sheer cliffs, the whole ride kept the noisy, upbeat energy of a holiday convoy.

## Jade water & travertine ledges

Nothing prepares you for the color of Cao Bang's water in person — mineral-rich karst streams sliding cleanly over white stone weirs like liquid emerald beneath towering cliff walls.

## Scarlet against limestone green

Climbing the long terraced steps of the highland pagoda, every landing opened wider over the borderland valley. Red fabric against deep mountain jade created the boldest color contrast of the entire journey.
